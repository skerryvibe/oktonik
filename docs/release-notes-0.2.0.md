# OKTONIK 0.2.0

**Harmonic performance instrument · by Skerry Vibe**

Back up your Move projects before updating. Tested with Schwung 1.7.3.

## New

**Built-in sequencer.** Record chords, bass and melody into clips that play in
sync with Move, and edit them on the step buttons.

- Eight clips each for chords, bass and melody, with their own length and rate
- Live recording, overdub while Move plays, undo and part mute
- Count-in and metronome
- Step editing with Move's gestures: length, nudge, copy, loop length
- Shift + step shortcuts in Move's layout
- Per-clip quantize, non-destructive

**Extension profiles.** Twelve styles, from POP and CLAS to JAZZ and AMB, give
each chord in the scale its own extension in one move.

**Editable modifier pads.** Choose what each modifier pad does, from approach
chords to added tones such as 6/9, b9 or #11.

**More chord extensions** per pad: 6/9, 7b5, 7b9, 7#9, 7#11, 7b13, maj9#11,
13(no11) and 5.

- PENTA and BLUES melody palettes
- Right-hand bass pads (R.PAD)
- Strum menu: direction, timing and velocity variation
- Illustrated guide included as GUIDE.html

## Changed

- New navigation: Track 1–3 open CHORD, BASS and MELODY; Menu opens the clip view
- LEAD is OFF, CNTR or FREE; WIDE spread is narrower and clearer
- Held melody notes keep sounding through chord changes
- Bass follows pad velocity; Shift + step 10 gives fixed full velocity
- Lock BORROW with its pad + main encoder (was Shift + BORROW)
- 0.1.0 projects keep their settings; going back to 0.1.0 can lose clips

## Install

Download **oktonik-module.tar.gz** below, upload it at `move.local:7700` and
restart Move. See the [guide](https://github.com/skerryvibe/oktonik/blob/main/docs/oktonik-public.md) for everything else.
